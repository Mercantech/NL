Packing

Clothes for school and trips, rain jacket, medicine, passport and EU health insurance card. The hotel provides towels and bedding — no need to pack those. Danish packing checklist →

Money & phone

The school covers transport, hotel, hotel breakfast and entrance fees for planned activities. Bring pocket money for lunch, dinner and personal spending. EU roaming usually applies — check your plan.

Expectations

Join in, stay open to language and culture, and follow the teachers’ guidance. Evenings are free — tell the group on WhatsApp if you go further away. At gatherings we count aloud in order (tællerække): 1, then 2, then 3… so nobody is missing.
