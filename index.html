Viborg · Denmark

Programme in Denmark

Standard school hours in Viborg are 09:00–14:30.

09:00 – Morning meeting on the third floor, followed by time for a daily stand-up with your project group.
11:30 – Lunch together.
14:15 – On regular school days, we meet again to wrap up.

Theory starts at 10:00 on the first four school days, 30 November–3 December. You can join the theory sessions online.

Project work: The railway, DMX lightshow and projection mapping groups stay on the third floor. All other groups work in Datahouse.

Lunch is from 11:30 to 12:00. The other breaks are flexible, usually 09:30–10:00 and 13:30–13:45. Ask the Danish students when your group will take those breaks.

Where Day Date Weekday Programme
DK Day 1 29 Nov Sunday
Arrival and a quiet evening

The group arrives late. Settle in at Mercantec Kollegiet and get a good night’s sleep. Save the Christmas market and cathedral for another day.

DK Day 2 30 Nov Monday

10:00 · Unit testing in C#
We compare unit, integration and end-to-end tests, then write a small test in xUnit or NUnit using Arrange–Act–Assert. We look at how a mock replaces a dependency so the test can focus on one behaviour.

14:30 · End of the school day

Ice skating near Borgvold

14:30 Walk together from school to the ice rink near Borgvold. Return before dinner.

DK Day 3 1 Dec Tuesday

10:00 · Integration testing
We test how components work together. For example, we can send a request to an API, let it read or save data in a test database and check the response.

14:30 · End of the school day

Free afternoon

Visit the Christmas market at Nytorv until 19:00, play board games at school, watch a film together or go to a café or bar. Make plans on WhatsApp.

DK Day 4 2 Dec Wednesday

10:00 · End-to-end testing with Bruno
We build an API test collection around a complete flow, such as signing in and calling a protected endpoint. We check successful responses and error cases, then look at how the team can share and rerun the tests.

14:30 · End of the school day

Space Jump

Meet downstairs at Mercantec Kollegiet at 14:45. Trampolines and arcade from 16:00 to 19:00. Cost DKK 30 per person. Alternatively, meet other students at Ølluminati from around 16:00 for non-alcoholic drinks and board games too.

DK Day 5 3 Dec Thursday

10:00 · GitHub Actions
We look at where continuous integration and deployment fit into CI/CD. We set up a workflow that builds the project and runs tests automatically, then use the status and logs to find out why a check failed.

14:30 · End of the school day

Beer Walk and board games

The Viborg Beer Walk costs DKK 150 per person for six small beers and a glass to keep. Route to follow. Ølluminati is a possible meeting point before or after. Board games at Datahouse around 14:00–20:00.

DK Day 6 4 Dec Friday

Friday café on the third floor with snacks, games and drinks. Around 10:30, time to be confirmed.

14:30 · End of the school day

Departure for Aarhus

The Dutch group leaves for Aarhus in the afternoon.

DK Day 7 5 Dec Saturday
Den Gamle By and free time in Aarhus

11:00 Visit Den Gamle By. Danish students are welcome and cover their own train or bus. Free time afterwards, with Aarhus Street Food as an option.

DK Day 8 6 Dec Sunday
Aarhus and return to Viborg

Free time in Aarhus. The Dutch group returns to Viborg in the evening. Details to follow.

DK Day 9 7 Dec Monday

14:30 · End of the school day

Possible after-school activities

Ideas for the afternoon include a football match in Viborg (possibly 10 tickets), a photo scavenger hunt or cooking Christmas food together. Activity still to be decided.

DK Day 10 8 Dec Tuesday

14:30 · End of the school day

Free afternoon

Christmas market at Nytorv until 19:00, board games, a film, bowling or laser tag. Make plans on WhatsApp.

DK Day 11 9 Dec Wednesday

12:00 · Joint presentations on the third floor
All project groups take part. Presentations continue until 14:30.

14:30 · End of the school day

Optional meet-up at Ølluminati

Suggestion: meet other Mercantec students at Ølluminati around 16:00.

DK Day 12 10 Dec Thursday

14:30 · End of the school day

Jam Night

Jam Night at Paletten starts at 19:00.

DK Day 13 11 Dec Friday
Farewell in Viborg

The Dutch group heads home. See the travel overview for journey details.
