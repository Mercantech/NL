For Danish students · TerAA × Mercantec Colab

Learning objectives

What you are expected to learn and do during the exchange — in international teams, with IoT and real project work in both countries.

Purpose

What the programme is for

  • Work in international teams and develop IoT projects together with Dutch students.
  • Apply your skills in programming, web development, API technologies and database systems.
  • Use Arduino for IoT projects and integrate it with other technologies.

Activities

How you will work

  • You are placed in mixed groups of about 2–3 Danish and 2–3 Dutch students.
  • Each group collaborates on developing an IoT project.
  • You use GitHub for collaboration and apply elements from SCRUM in the project work.
  • The programme includes time in both the Netherlands and Denmark.

Expected outcomes

What you take with you

  • Experience in international collaboration and cross-cultural communication.
  • Practical skills in IoT and programming from real project work.
  • The chance to shape the programme and give input for future exchanges — the goal is for this to become an annual event, potentially across all H3 courses.

Follow these objectives throughout the exchange — in Helmond, in Viborg, and in your group's IoT project.
